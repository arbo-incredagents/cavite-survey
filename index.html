Good day! We are building a website to help people find businesses in Naic. Each business gets a simple page, called a listing, that shows its name, what it offers, and where it is.

This preview shows four questions about a free listing, usefulness, concerns, and a possible paid upgrade. There is no commitment to join or pay.

A listing currently shows the business name, category, a short description, and location. It may also include a website or Google Maps link when available. Phone numbers, email addresses, and photos are not shown in the public directory.

Need a listing corrected or removed? Message the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page with the business name and the change you are requesting. This survey does not change or remove directory listings; requests are reviewed before any listing is changed.
