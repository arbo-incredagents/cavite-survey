Cavite Business Connect

Survey Privacy Notice

IncredAgents is responsible for the business research survey conducted through Cavite Business Connect. Survey collection is active, and participation is voluntary. You may review this notice and decide whether to take part before submitting answers.

What the survey asks and why

The form asks four questions about interest in a free listing, usefulness of the directory, concerns, and a possible monthly paid option. It also has an optional business-name field and optional free-text answers. The form does not ask for your personal name, email address, or phone number. Please do not include sensitive personal information, another person's information, or confidential business information in a text answer.

A submitted answer record will include the answers you provide, a generated response ID, the date and time recorded, and the survey, notice, and consent version identifiers. We use answers to research the Naic directory idea and may prepare summaries that do not identify respondents. We do not publish identifiable answers, disclose them to other businesses, add respondents to a marketing list, or use survey answers to automatically change a business listing. We do not download response copies.

Who hosts the survey and answers

GitHub hosts this survey and records visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is separate from survey answers and is handled under GitHub’s privacy policies.

When you submit the form, your answers are sent securely to our private survey service on DigitalOcean servers in the United States. To help prevent repeated or abusive requests, the service briefly checks your IP address but does not add it to your answer record. Your written answers are not included in the server’s activity records. DigitalOcean may handle information needed to run and protect its hosting service.

To notify the project operator when a response arrives, the survey service sends Google Apps Script a generated alert-event identifier, a timestamp, and a security signature. Google Apps Script uses this limited metadata to verify the alert request and send a generic email to arbo@incredagents.com. The alert does not include your survey answers, business name, response ID, or dashboard credentials. Google processes the alert metadata and email delivery information under its own privacy policy.

If you message the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page, Meta handles your message and account information under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep answers

The project's retention period is 90 days after the survey is formally closed. The response service is configured to delete identifiable answers and linked response-administration records after that period. If the service is unavailable at the deletion time, the deletion runs when it next starts or handles a request. This 90-day period is a project choice, not a statutory default. Non-identifying summaries may be kept for research. GitHub security logs, Meta messages, and provider operational records follow their providers' own retention practices.

Your choices and privacy rights

Taking part is optional. You may leave without submitting. Where applicable, you may ask whether we process your personal information, request access or correction, object to processing, withdraw your consent, seek erasure or blocking when the legal conditions apply, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC). These rights may be subject to legal limits.

For a survey privacy request, message the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page. The inbox is monitored Tuesday to Saturday, 6:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Manila time (UTC+8). We aim to acknowledge requests within five inbox coverage days; this is an internal acknowledgment target. We will handle requests without undue delay and within 30 working days after receiving the request and any necessary supporting information. For complex or numerous requests, NPC Advisory No. 2021-01 allows one extension of up to 15 additional working days if we tell you the reason. You can read the NPC explanation of data-subject rights or contact the NPC.

Questions about a directory listing

This notice and survey concern survey answers, not directory-listing changes. The project has not opened a separate formal listing-correction or removal service. To ask what options are available for a listing, message the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page with the business name and the change or removal you are asking about. A message is a request for guidance; it does not confirm that a listing will be changed or removed. This survey does not submit listing requests.

Consent

Before submitting, you will be asked separately to confirm that you have read this notice and to voluntarily agree to the use of your answers for the research described above. Reading the notice and agreeing to participate are separate choices.
