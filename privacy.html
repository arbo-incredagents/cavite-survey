Cavite Business Connect

Survey Privacy Notice

IncredAgents is responsible for the business research survey conducted through Cavite Business Connect. Participation is voluntary.

What we collect and why

Your survey answers will be used only to research local businesses in Naic and improve the Cavite Business Connect directory. We will not publish or share your answers with other people or businesses.

Only Tina Marie, CEO of IncredAgents, and Christian Joseph Arbo, the local team member, can access survey answers for review. We will not download copies of responses.

Where information is processed

The survey form is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information for site security and may process information in locations outside the Philippines.

Survey answers are intended to be stored on a DigitalOcean server in its SFO3 region in the United States. DigitalOcean processes the data needed to provide that hosting service.

If you contact us about privacy through the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page, Meta will process that message under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep answers

We plan to delete identifiable survey answers and their linked administrative records 90 days after the survey closes. Provider security or service logs may be kept under the providers' own retention practices.

Your choice and privacy requests

Taking part is optional. You may decline to answer or stop before submitting. To ask about your information or make a privacy request, message the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page. The inbox is monitored Tuesday to Saturday, 6:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Manila time (UTC+8). We aim to respond within the next five coverage days.

Correcting or removing a directory listing

If you see a listing with incorrect information or want a business listing removed, message the Cavite Business Connect Facebook Page. Include the business name and the correction or removal you are requesting. We review requests before changing a listing. The survey does not submit listing changes or removal requests.

Consent

Before submitting, you will be asked to confirm that you have read this notice and voluntarily agree to the use of your answers for the research described above.
